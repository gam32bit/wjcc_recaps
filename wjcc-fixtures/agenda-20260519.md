_**BACKGROUND:**_

In accordance with the school board approved budget calendar, the superintendent presented his FY2027 Proposed Budget to the school board on February 3, 2026. The school board approved its budget on March 3, 2026. The School Board Approved Budget was then brought before the James City County Board of Supervisors and the Williamsburg City Council for consideration. 

 

The School Board Approved FY27 budget request included an above and beyond request of the localities in the amount of $1,225,000 which is not being funded. With the appropriating bodies planned action on the FY27 budget, there are reductions and adjustments that need to occur in order to balance the budget.  To balance the FY27 budget, $1,225,000 was reduced from the operating fund.

 

_**RATIONALE:**_

The superintendent presented the recommended budget proposal for the school board’s consideration.   [VA §22.1-92](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title22.1/chapter8/section22.1-92/), _Estimate of  __moneys_ _  needed for public schools; notice of costs to be distributed_ states, “It shall be the duty of each division superintendent to prepare, with the approval of the school board, and submit to the governing body or bodies appropriating funds for the school division, by the date specified in §[15.2-2503](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title15.2/chapter25/section15.2-2503/), the estimate of the amount of money deemed to be needed during the next fiscal year for the support of the public schools of the school division. The estimate shall set up the amount of money deemed to be needed for each major classification prescribed by the Board of Education and such other headings or items as may be necessary.”

 

The superintendent is charged to prepare the estimate of the amount of funds deemed to be needed during the next fiscal year for the support of the schools in the school division, pursuant to [§](http://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title22.1/chapter8/section22.1-92/)[22.1-92](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title22.1/chapter8/section22.1-92/) of the Code of Virginia. Subsequently, the school board is charged with the responsibility to propose, and ultimately adopt, a division budget.

 

_**SPECIAL CIRCUMSTANCES:**_

Once the state budget is finalized, the school board may need to amend the FY27 Budget if additional resources are allocated.

Three schools, to include Matthew Whaley Elementary, Norge Elementary, and James River Elementary have locations that prevent service signals reaching some interior locations from any of the service providers.

Beginning in February of this year, the technology department conducted testing using T-Mobile smartphones and Passpoint technology with satisfactory results at all locations.  The service also provides an option for DialPad, which provides mobile service via an app and web-based portal, allowing users to work from any mobile device or desktop.

  * 220 Smart phones ($29.40/month each)
  * 100 DialPad subscriptions ($21.00/month each)
  * 15 Staff mobile hotspots ($29.40/month each)
  * 40 Student mobile hotspots ($15.00/month each)



 

Total annual subscription is estimated to be $127,539.60.  This total will vary slightly year to year as staff and student needs change.
