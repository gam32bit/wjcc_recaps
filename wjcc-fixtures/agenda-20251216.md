_**TOPIC:**_

Redistricting Process Update

 

_**BACKGROUND:**_

Many school buildings in WJCC Schools, specifically at the elementary level, are at capacity. To accommodate student population, eight of nine WJCC elementary schools have portable classrooms (trailers) on site. As WJCC Schools prepares for the construction and anticipated August 2027 occupancy of two new pre-k centers, the school division must also consider the best and most efficient way to utilize elementary space that will be available when pre-k students are located in the new pre-k spaces as the current pre-k program is housed in five elementary schools. The shift will leave some elementary spaces unoccupied while other spaces remain at or over capacity. Engaging in conversation on school zones also allows the school division to consider efficiencies and effective delivery at the secondary level. 

 

Each year, school divisions submit enrollment reports, or the September 30th Count, to the state. With this process complete, MGT has received the most recent official WJCC report and is prepared to present enrollment data, trends, projections, and enrollment distribution by geography, and engage the School Board in continued conversation around the redistricting guiding principles and next steps in the process. 

 

_**RATIONALE:**_

MGT, a qualified and experienced consulting firm with background in demographic study and developing school zone options, will join administration in engaging the school board in dialogue around guiding principles for redistricting. With school board direction, MGT will analyze student forecast data and consider boundary scenarios. MGT and WJCC leadership will engage community stakeholders throughout 2026 leading to the school board's approval of a final boundary recommendation in October 2026 for implementation in August 2027 for the 2027-2028 and successive school years.

_**TOPIC:**_

Fiscal Year 2027 Operating Budget Overview

 

_**BACKGROUND:**_

Per the Contract for the Funding of the Joint School System for James City County and Williamsburg City, the School Board agreed that the County's FY 2027 annual contribution to the operational costs of the School System of not less than $104,500,000.00, and the City's FY 2027 annual contribution to the operational costs of the School System of not less than $14,450,000.00, is a reasonable amount to support the School System.  Following receipt of the localities' estimates for their annual contributions to the operational costs of the School System for the following fiscal year, the School Board shall work to develop a needs-based budget within those estimates.

 

Should the School Board determine that it requires additional funding for the operational costs of the School System in excess of the amounts the localities have provided in their annual operating contribution estimates, they may request an increase in the funding from the County and City in writing specifically (i) asking to receive more than the allocation provides, (ii) describing what the additional funding will be used for, (iii) indicating whether the request is recurring, and (iv) if needed, supporting by resolution, the raising of real property tax or other taxes in the County and/or the City to support their request.

 

Per school board established budget timeline, after three budget work sessions in January, the superintendent will present the Proposed Budget on February 3, 2026.  The school board approves its budget proposal in March; it is then brought before the county board of supervisors and the city council. The localities must approve the appropriation for the school division no later than May 15. Following the approval of total budget appropriation, the school board makes any necessary adjustments to their budget proposal and adopts a final budget.

 

**_RATIONALE:_**

The superintendent will present the recommended budget proposal for the school board’s consideration in February 2026. VA [§ 22.1-92](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title22.1/chapter8/section22.1-92/). Estimate of moneys needed for public schools; notice of costs to be distributed states, “It shall be the duty of each division superintendent to prepare, with the approval of the school board, and submit to the governing body or bodies appropriating funds for the school division, by the date specified in [§](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title15.2/chapter25/section15.2-2503/) [15.2-2503](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title15.2/chapter25/section15.2-2503/), the estimate of the amount of money deemed to be needed during the next fiscal year for the support of the public schools of the school division. The estimate shall set up the amount of money deemed to be needed for each major classification prescribed by the Board of Education and such other headings or items as may be necessary.”

 

Taking into consideration the new contract and budget timeline changes for Fiscal Year 2027, an overview of the operating budget will be presented to the school board prior to the January budget work sessions.

**_TOPIC:_**

School Board Annual Self-Evaluation

 

**_POLICY ALIGNMENT:_**  

[AFA, Evaluation of School Board Operational Procedures](https://go.boarddocs.com/vsba/wjcc/Board.nsf/goto?open&id=DBDHNZ49633E)

 

**_BACKGROUND:_**

The school board believes the efficiency of board operations directly affects the efficiency of division operations. Therefore, the board will conduct an annual self-evaluation in December.

 

**_RATIONALE:_**

The evaluation will assess the effectiveness of the school board in the discharge of its role, both individually and as a board. The following conditions shall apply to the school board self-evaluation process:

  * School board members shall be involved in the development of an instrument by which they will evaluate themselves.
  * The school board evaluation will be completed by individual board members confidentially, and submitted to the school board chair or designee, for compilation of individual results.
  * The school board shall meet, with a quorum of members present, to review and discuss the composite opinions and total results.
  * Each judgment shall be supported with as much rationale and objective evidence as possible.



**_SPECIAL CIRCUMSTANCE(S):_**

Upon completion of the annual process, the school board will consider revisions to the school board self-evaluation process.

**_BACKGROUND:_**

In the WJCC Strategic Plan, _Elevate '28_ , Organizational Efficiency & Effectiveness is a priority and guides the work of the Operations Department with respect to optimizing processes.  To support this priority, the Operations team has worked to determine the feasibility of using solar energy for WJCC Schools and facilities. During the analysis, the division determined that partnering with a solar provider would create educational and learning opportunities for students, improve sustainability efforts by reducing energy consumption, and create a cost savings for the school division.

 

WJCC requested proposals from qualified firms, partnerships, corporations, associations or professional organizations to provide turn-key solar Photovoltaics (PV) services through a Power Purchase Agreement (PPA). The division is willing to review other financing and ownership models including a prepaid PPA, a conventional PPA with tax benefits through system donation, a lease, or any other structured proposal. WJCC believes on-site PV power generation will provide a long-term financial benefit by reducing energy costs through reduction of peak demand loads and daily energy consumption at the sites. The program will provide high quality power with minimal environmental impact. The PV System will be a rooftop application on viable WJCC administrative and school buildings.

 

An RFP was issued on March 25, 2025, with a due date of May 6, 2025. Notice of the RFP was posted on the James City County Bulletin Board, emailed to Contractors, and placed on the James City County website.  

 

**_RATIONALE:_**

Seven vendors responded to the RFP.  Of those, Dominion Energy was chosen by the Selection Committee as the respondent that met the needs of WJCC Schools.

 

**_SPECIAL CIRCUMSTANCE(S):_**

N/A

 

**_COST BUDGETED:_**

There is no cost to WJCCS for installation or maintenance of the solar panels and system. The only cost to WJCC is the cost of purchasing the energy produced by the solar system installed at the respective buildings/facilities.

 

**_ALTERNATIVES:_**

2\. Do not approve the superintendent’s recommendation.

 

**_SUPERINTENDENT’S RECOMMENDATION:_**

The superintendent recommends that the board approve the award of contract for Request for Proposal #20250927 Solar Power Purchase Agreement to Dominion Energy.
