TOPIC:

Appointment of School Board Members to Advisory and Standing Committees

 

BACKGROUND:

Each year the school board appoints members to the represent the school board on the following advisory and standing committees:

  • Career Ready Advisory Committee - Charged to review and advise on the division-wide career/technical programs and curricula for students as well as staff training identified by the business community.  One member is appointed.
  • Special Education Advisory Committee - Charged to further the education of students with disabilities through participation in general education programs, advocacy, communication and community education.  One member is appointed.
  • Policy Committee - Charged to review draft policies before they are brought before the school board.  Two members are appointed.
  • School Liaison Committee - Charged to share information with two city council members and two county board of supervisors members.  Two members are appointed.
  • School Safety Audit Committee - Charged to review the completed school safety audits and submit any plans, as needed, for improving school safety to the division superintendent for submission to the school board.  One member is appointed.
  • Mentor Teacher Program Committee - Charged to review and evaluate the effectiveness of the mentor program.  One member is appointed.

 

RATIONALE:

Policy BCF, School Board Committees, includes the appointment of members of the school board to each advisory or standing committee as appropriate.

TOPIC:

Approve Amendment to the Calendar Year 2026 School Board Meeting Dates

 

BACKGROUND:

Policy BCA, Board Organizational Meeting, requires the board to establish the dates, time, and locations of all regular meetings for the ensuing year at its Organizational Meeting.  In September 2025, it was determined that more advance planning and notice of the meeting schedule increases efficiency and improves communication for board members, staff, and the community.  The school board then approved the meeting schedule for calendar year 2026 with the intention to either confirm or amend the planned meeting dates at the January Organizational Meeting in order to remain compliant with state code.

 

RATIONALE:

The State Code of Virginia, § 22.1-72, reads: "Each school board shall hold its annual organizational meeting for the purpose of establishing its regular meeting schedule for the ensuing year..."

 

A Special Call Meeting will be added to June 30, 2026 at 4 p.m. to approve Personnel Actions.

 

SPECIAL CIRCUMSTANCE:

The school board may also hold special meetings when necessary.

Goals
Key Priority: Academic Achievement, College and Career Readiness & Educational Equity - Transform teaching and learning to prepare students for success in post-secondary education and careers.
Supporting Priority: Communications & Engagement - Cultivate a culture of open and effective communication to inform and engage all stakeholders.
Supporting Priority: Safety & Security - Ensure a fully integrated approach to safety and security that encompasses both the physical environment and the social/emotional needs of students.

TOPIC:Kindergarten Orientation Model

BACKGROUND:A successful transition into kindergarten is supported by a comprehensive orientation that enables parents/guardians to become knowledgeable about the kindergarten experience and facilitates the school staff's opportunities to understand students’ needs.  This model prioritizes meaningful engagement, observation, informed decision making, and planning to ensure student needs may be addressed.

RATIONALE:Current registration and class assignment processes limit opportunities to gather comprehensive information about incoming Kindergarteners.  The Kindergarten Orientation Model will provide a proactive approach which aims to create balanced classrooms, strengthen early relationships with families, and increase long-term student success.
