To promote awareness of school bullying, the Virginia School Boards Association of Directors has set aside the month of October as Bullying Prevention Awareness Month.

 

RATIONALE:

Childhood bullying is a significant problem nationwide. It can cause school absenteeism, mental and physical stress, poor school performance, and poor self-esteem. School board members, superintendents, teachers, and parents can play a critical role in creating a climate where bullying is not tolerated. Each October, WJCC Public Schools supports Bullying Prevention Month through a variety of activities. The division website is utilized to provide information on bullying prevention and the October observance. Each school plans specific activities to acknowledge Bullying Prevention Month.

TOPIC:

Approve Resolution R-19-25, National School Lunch Week

 

BACKGROUND:

First declared in 1962, the annual observance of National School Lunch Week promotes the importance of a healthy school lunch in a child's life and the impact it has inside and outside of the classroom.  

 

The National School Lunch Program (NSLP) serves nearly 30 million children every school day to ensure the health and well-being of all public school students, boost academic performance by giving students the fuel they need to succeed, and closed the opportunity gap for children across the country.  The program reduces childhood hunger and also supports American agriculture.

 

RATIONALE:

The WJCC Child Nutrition Services Program provides lunch and breakfast to students daily, while adhering to strict nutrition standards and navigating student food allergies. Research demonstrates a strong relationship between nutrition and readiness to learn. The meals served to students provide the energy and nutrients for optimal cognitive development and performance. Williamsburg-James City County has exceptional Child Nutrition Services staff members in each of the sixteen schools, and the division is proud to recognize the difference school nutrition professionals make for every child who comes through the cafeteria. 

 

The week of October 13-17, 2025, is recognized as National School Lunch Week.

Approve the Award of Contract for Request for Proposal #20250543, Plagiarism Prevention and AI Detection Software to CopyLeaks for an Initial Two-year Contract Cost of $43,435.50

 

POLICY ALIGNMENT:

DJ, Purchasing 

DJ-R, Purchasing Regulation

 

BACKGROUND: 

The Williamsburg-James City County Public Schools (WJCC) solicited sealed proposals to establish a contract through competitive negotiation for a plagiarism prevention and artificial intelligence (AI) detection tool for grades 6-12 that supports academic institutions in determining if students are writing and submitting original work. The program will provide educators with a tool that searches the Internet, as well as databases of papers and digital texts for sources of potential plagiarism. The tool would then generate a custom report that highlights and provides links to any textual matches found on the Internet, in databases of previously submitted papers, and/or in proprietary databases of subscription-based publication material. The desire of this program is to support the schools’ efforts for identifying academic integrity and to provide teachers with the resources to have conversations about academic writing.  

 

Requested goods and/or services (defined in detail in the RFP) include:

  1. Online Originality Detective Service
  2. Similarity Search Database
  3. Similarity Report
  4. Formative Writing Tool
  5. System Reporting
  6. Certain Technical Requirements
  7. Data Privacy and Security
  8. Product Training and Support
  9. AI Detection Requirements

 

The proposals were reviewed based on the following evaluation criteria:

 

  • Vendor Qualifications, Experience / References
  • Demonstrated understanding of the Statement of Needs and ability to meet RFP requirements
  • Functionality of the Software
  • Proposed Work Plan that demonstrates the firm’s ability to provide services
  • Cost

The contract will be for an initial two (2) year period and eight (8) optional one-year renewals.  

A request for proposal was issued on May 15, 2025, with a due date of June 12, 2025.  Notice of the RFP was posted on the James City County website, posted on the Commonwealth of Virginia eProcurement Portal website, and posted on Demandstar Procurement Portal website.  The RFP Committee evaluated the vendors' proposals and then met on July 24, 2025 to review vendor demonstrations. 

 

 

RATIONALE: 

To support academic integrity and responsible technology use, the school division initiated an RFP process to identify a plagiarism prevention and AI detection tool that meets the evolving needs of students and educators in grades 6–12. This ensures the selection of a high-quality, cost-effective solution aligned with instructional goals and data privacy standards.

 

School Board Policy DJ requires purchases in excess of $100,000.00 to be approved by the school board.

BCF, School Board Committees

 

BACKGROUND:

Each year the school board appoints citizens to be on School Board Advisory Committees:  Special Education, Student Advisory, and Career Ready (CTE).  These committees act in an advisory capacity only.  The school board will determine the number of members to serve on any advisory committee and shall appoint the members of the committee.  Membership on committees shall be in accordance with state and federal laws and regulations and reflects, whenever possible, the diversity of the community.  Members of the WJCC Schools administrative staff shall serve only as resource persons and liaisons to the advisory committees.

 

RATIONALE:

Board Policy BCF, School Board Committees, lists the operating procedures to govern the operations of advisory committees, unless otherwise provided by the school board or by state or federal law or regulations.  Unless prohibited by federal or state law, a minimum of one school board member is appointed to each committee to represent the board and serve as a consultant to the committee.  An advisory committee appointment term is two years.  Members of an advisory committee may be appointed for a second consecutive two year term.  Members are ineligible to serve for more than two consecutive terms. Appointments to advisory committees will be made by the first October school board meeting of each year.  The school board may make additional appointments to an advisory committee during the school year.  After committees are formed, any additional applications received will be saved for review and consideration either in the event of mid-year vacancies, or for possible appointment the following school year.

 

SPECIAL CIRCUMSTANCE(S):

The Student Advisory Committee appointments are for up to two years and end on June 30 of the school year they are appointed.
