Recommended Action
Move to adopt the fiscal year 2027 budget in the total amount of $219,020,000.
This includes $201,490,000 for the operating fund; $7,100,000 for the grants fund; $ 1,337,000 for the state operated programs fund; and $9,093,000 for the child nutrition services fund.
Goals
Key Priority: Academic Achievement, College and Career Readiness & Educational Equity - Transform teaching and learning to prepare students for success in post-secondary education and careers.
Supporting Priority: Human Capital & Positive Culture - Recruit and retain the division’s exemplary workforce by creating an environment where transparency and trust are the norm.
Supporting Priority: Organizational Efficiency & Effectiveness - Optimize division effectiveness and efficiency by establishing and strengthening processes and systems.
Supporting Priority: Safety & Security - Ensure a fully integrated approach to safety and security that encompasses both the physical environment and the social/emotional needs of students.
Supporting Priority: Communications & Engagement - Cultivate a culture of open and effective communication to inform and engage all stakeholders.

TOPIC:

Approve the Adoption of the Fiscal Year 2027 Budget in the Amount of $219,020,000.

 

POLICY ALIGNMENT:

BBA, School Board Powers and Duties

DB, Annual Budget

 

BACKGROUND:

In accordance with the school board approved budget calendar, the superintendent presented his FY2027 Proposed Budget to the school board on February 3, 2026. The school board approved its budget on March 3, 2026. The School Board Approved Budget was then brought before the James City County Board of Supervisors and the Williamsburg City Council for consideration. 

 

The School Board Approved FY27 budget request included an above and beyond request of the localities in the amount of $1,225,000 which is not being funded. With the appropriating bodies planned action on the FY27 budget, there are reductions and adjustments that need to occur in order to balance the budget.  To balance the FY27 budget, $1,225,000 was reduced from the operating fund.

 

RATIONALE:

The superintendent presented the recommended budget proposal for the school board’s consideration.   VA §22.1-92, Estimate of moneys needed for public schools; notice of costs to be distributed states, “It shall be the duty of each division superintendent to prepare, with the approval of the school board, and submit to the governing body or bodies appropriating funds for the school division, by the date specified in §15.2-2503, the estimate of the amount of money deemed to be needed during the next fiscal year for the support of the public schools of the school division. The estimate shall set up the amount of money deemed to be needed for each major classification prescribed by the Board of Education and such other headings or items as may be necessary.”

 

The superintendent is charged to prepare the estimate of the amount of funds deemed to be needed during the next fiscal year for the support of the schools in the school division, pursuant to §22.1-92 of the Code of Virginia. Subsequently, the school board is charged with the responsibility to propose, and ultimately adopt, a division budget.

 

SPECIAL CIRCUMSTANCES:

Once the state budget is finalized, the school board may need to amend the FY27 Budget if additional resources are allocated.

 

ALTERNATIVES:

1. Adopt the FY27 Budget.

2. Do not adopt the FY27 Budget.

 

SUPERINTENDENT’S RECOMMENDATION:

The superintendent recommends that the school board adopt the amended FY27 Budget.

$     7,100,000       (Grants Fund)

BACKGROUND:

Adoption of the Pay Scales and Classification Plan for Fiscal Year 2026-2027. It is the practice of the Williamsburg and James City County Public Schools to establish and maintain a fair and uniform human resources management system for its employees in order to ensure the most effective delivery of services to the students and citizens of the City of Williamsburg and James City County.
