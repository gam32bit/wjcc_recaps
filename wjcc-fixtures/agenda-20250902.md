_**POLICY ALIGNMENT:**_

[DJ, Purchasing ](http://go.boarddocs.com/vsba/wjcc/Board.nsf/goto?open&id=CE9P72632C54)

**_BACKGROUND:_**  

The Williamsburg-James City County Public Schools (WJCC) solicited sealed proposals to establish a contract through competitive negotiation for a plagiarism prevention and artificial intelligence (AI) detection tool for grades 6-12 that supports academic institutions in determining if students are writing and submitting original work. The program will provide educators with a tool that searches the Internet, as well as databases of papers and digital texts for sources of potential plagiarism. The tool would then generate a custom report that highlights and provides links to any textual matches found on the Internet, in databases of previously submitted papers, and/or in proprietary databases of subscription-based publication material. The desire of this program is to support the schools’ efforts for identifying academic integrity and to provide teachers with the resources to have conversations about academic writing.  

 

Requested goods and/or services (defined in detail in the RFP) include:

  1. Online Originality Detective Service
  2. Similarity Search Database
  3. Similarity Report
  4. Formative Writing Tool
  5. System Reporting
  6. Certain Technical Requirements
  7. Data Privacy and Security
  8. Product Training and Support
  9. AI Detection Requirements



 

The proposals were reviewed based on the following evaluation criteria:

 

  * Vendor Qualifications, Experience / References
  * Demonstrated understanding of the Statement of Needs and ability to meet RFP requirements
  * Functionality of the Software
  * Proposed Work Plan that demonstrates the firm’s ability to provide services
  * Cost



The contract will be for an initial two (2) year period and eight (8) optional one year renewals.  

A request for proposal was issued on May 15, 2025, with a due date of June 12, 2025.  Notice of the RFP was posted on the James City County website, posted on the Commonwealth of Virginia eProcurement Portal website, and posted on Demandstar Procurement Portal website.  The RFP Committee evaluated the vendors' proposals and then met on July 24, 2025 to review vendor demonstrations. 

 

 

_**RATIONALE:  **_

To support academic integrity and responsible technology use, the school division initiated an RFP process to identify a plagiarism prevention and AI detection tool that meets the evolving needs of students and educators in grades 6–12. This ensures the selection of a high-quality, cost-effective solution aligned with instructional goals and data privacy standards.

 

School Board Policy DJ requires purchases in excess of $100,000.00 to be approved by the school board.

The initial two year contract cost is $43,435.50. Funds for this expense are budgeted each year in the operating fund within the Instructional Technology cost center.
