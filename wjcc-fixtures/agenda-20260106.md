_**TOPIC:**_

Appointment of School Board Members to Advisory and Standing Committees

 

_**BACKGROUND:**_

Each year the school board appoints members to the represent the school board on the following advisory and standing committees: 

  * **Career Ready Advisory Committee** \- Charged to review and advise on the division-wide career/technical programs and curricula for students as well as staff training identified by the business community.  One member is appointed.
  * **Special Education Advisory Committee** \- Charged to further the education of students with disabilities through participation in general education programs, advocacy, communication and community education.  One member is appointed.
  * **Policy Committee**  \- Charged to review draft policies before they are brought before the school board.  Two members are appointed.
  * **School Liaison Committee** \- Charged to share information with two city council members and two county board of supervisors members.  Two members are appointed.
  * **School Safety Audit Committee -** Charged to review the completed school safety audits and submit any plans, as needed, for improving school safety to the division superintendent for submission to the school board.  One member is appointed.
  * **Mentor Teacher Program Committee -** Charged to review and evaluate the effectiveness of the mentor program. ** ** One member is appointed.



 

_**RATIONALE:**_

[Policy BCF, School Board Committees](https://go.boarddocs.com/vsba/wjcc/Board.nsf/goto?open&id=D85HXX4AB0FF), includes the appointment of members of the school board to each advisory or standing committee as appropriate.

**_TOPIC:_**

Approve Amendment to the Calendar Year 2026 School Board Meeting Dates

 

**_BACKGROUND:_**

[Policy BCA, Board Organizational Meeting](http://go.boarddocs.com/vsba/wjcc/Board.nsf/goto?open&id=CG3RL96E7BC7), requires the board to establish the dates, time, and locations of all regular meetings for the ensuing year at its Organizational Meeting.  In September 2025, it was determined that more advance planning and notice of the meeting schedule increases efficiency and improves communication for board members, staff, and the community.  The school board then approved the meeting schedule for calendar year 2026 with the intention to either confirm or amend the planned meeting dates at the January Organizational Meeting in order to remain compliant with state code.

 

**_RATIONALE:_**

The State Code of Virginia, [§](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title22.1/chapter7/section22.1-72/)[ 22.1-72](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title22.1/chapter7/section22.1-72/), reads: "Each school board shall hold its annual organizational meeting for the purpose of establishing its regular meeting schedule for the ensuing year..."

 

A Special Call Meeting will be added to June 30, 2026 at 4 p.m. to approve Personnel Actions.

 

**_SPECIAL CIRCUMSTANCE:_**

The school board may also hold special meetings when necessary.
