**_TOPIC:_**

Approve Resolution R-9-26, Month of the Military Child

 

**_BACKGROUND:_**

In 1986, Secretary of Defense Caspar W. Weinberger designated April as “The Month of the Military Child” in recognition of the contributions that military children make to the community and nation. During April, all branches of service, the Commonwealth and many school divisions provide special activities, events and days to honor military-connected children and their families.

 

**_RATIONALE:_**

Military children experience frequent moves, family separations as a result of deployments as well as reintegration challenges. Attending new schools, maintaining their educational goals and making new friends are the way of life for most of our military children. As they strive to excel under these challenging circumstances, our military children and their families deserve the support and recognition of our community and our nation.

**_TOPIC:_**

Approve Resolution R-10-26, School Library Month

 

**_BACKGROUND:_**

School Library Month is the American Association of School Librarians' (AASL) celebration of school librarians and their programs. First celebrated in 1985 (as School Library Media Month; the name was changed to School Library Month in 2010), every April school librarians are encouraged to create activities to help their school and local community celebrate the essential role that strong school library programs play in a student's educational career.

 

**_RATIONALE:_**

The American Association of School Librarians (AASL) defines the school library as “a unique and essential part of a learning community.” Effective school libraries deepen students’ learning experiences.  Under the leadership of the school librarian, the library provides equitable access for teachers and students to a variety of resources and technology.  During the month of April, school systems are reminded of the special significance that school library programs and school librarians have in Virginia’s schools.
