TOPIC:

Approve the Financial Report and Monthly Bills & Payroll for the Month Ended October 31, 2025

 

BACKGROUND:

The Finance Department prepares financial reports and a monthly bills & payroll report at the end of each month. These reports summarize the financial activities of the school division. The reports prepared include:

1.    Financial Highlights

2.    Budget Status Report – All Funds

3.    Expenditures by Cost Center – Operating Fund

4.    Monthly Bills & Payroll – All Funds

5.    Purchase Orders issued over $25,000

6.    Contracts Signed between $25,000 - $100,000

7.    Transfers Between Categories up to $25,000

 

RATIONALE:

The presentation of the monthly financial reports and monthly bills & payroll report ensures accountability to the board and the citizens of James City County and the City of Williamsburg of the financial resources entrusted to the school division.

TOPIC:

Fiscal Year 27-36 Capital Improvement Plan Discussion

 

BACKGROUND:

Per school board established budget timeline, the superintendent presents the FY27-36 Capital Improvement Plan (CIP) to the school board.

 

After the submission of the Superintendent’s Proposed CIP, the school board approves the CIP (in December). It is then presented to the James City County Board of Supervisors and Williamsburg City Council for consideration. The localities must approve the CIP and any appropriation for the school division no later than May 15th. Following the approval of the total budget appropriation, the school board makes any necessary adjustments and adopts a final CIP.

 

RATIONALE:

The superintendent presented his recommended CIP for the school board’s consideration on November 11, 2025. VA §22.1-92.. Estimate of moneys needed for public schools; notice of costs to be distributed states, “It shall be the duty of each division superintendent to prepare, with the approval of the school board, and submit to the governing body or bodies appropriating funds for the school division, by the date specified in VA §15.2-2503, the estimate of the amount of money deemed to be needed during the next fiscal year for the support of the public schools of the school division. The estimate shall set up the amount of money deemed to be needed for each major classification prescribed by the Board of Education and such other headings or items as may be necessary.”
