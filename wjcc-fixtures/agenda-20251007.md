_**BACKGROUND:**_

Each year the school board appoints citizens to be on the Special Education and Career Ready Advisory Committees.  These committees act in an advisory capacity only.  The school board will determine the number of members to serve on any advisory committee and shall appoint the members of the committee.  Membership on committees shall be in accordance with state and federal laws and regulations and reflects, whenever possible, the diversity of the community.  Members of the WJCC Schools administrative staff shall serve only as resource persons and liaisons to the advisory committees.

 

_**RATIONALE:**_

Board Policy BCF, _School Board Committees_ , lists the operating procedures to govern the operations of advisory committees, unless otherwise provided by the school board or by state or federal law or regulations.  Unless prohibited by federal or state law, a minimum of one school board member is appointed to each committee to represent the board and serve as a consultant to the committee.  An advisory committee appointment term is two years.  Members of an advisory committee may be appointed for a second consecutive two-year term.  Members are ineligible to serve for more than two consecutive terms. Appointments to advisory committees will be made by the first October school board meeting of each year.  The school board may make additional appointments to an advisory committee during the school year.  After committees are formed, any additional applications received will be saved for review and consideration either in the event of mid-year vacancies, or for possible appointment the following school year.
