Goals
Supporting Priority: Organizational Efficiency & Effectiveness - Optimize division effectiveness and efficiency by establishing and strengthening processes and systems.

TOPIC:

Review Purchase Request for Division Mobile Device Service from T-Mobile for a Total Annual Subscription Estimate of $127,539.60

 

POLICY ALIGNMENT:

DJ, Purchasing

DJ-R, Purchasing Regulations

 

BACKGROUND:

The division currently uses Verizon Wireless for mobile device services to include smart phones and mobile hotspots.  The service does have some problem areas in several schools where signal does not cover interior areas of some of the buildings.

 

Beginning in November of 2025, the department of technology began an analysis of the problem areas and surveyed coverage using several area service providers.  Three schools, to include Matthew Whaley Elementary, Norge Elementary, and James River Elementary have locations that prevent service signals reaching some interior locations from any of the service providers.

 

T-Mobile provides a capability, known as Passpoint, that allows their signal to be transmitted via our internal network wireless connections.  Beginning in February of this year, the technology department conducted testing using T-Mobile smartphones and Passpoint technology with satisfactory results at all locations.  The service also provides an option for DialPad, which provides mobile service via an app and web-based portal, allowing users to work from any mobile device or desktop.

 

The division currently subscribes for 320 smart phones, 15 staff mobile hotspots, and 40 student-use mobile hotspots for an annual cost of approximately $186,000 at an average cost of $45 per line.

 

This purchase to T-Mobile will provide:

  • 220 Smart phones ($29.40/month each)
  • 100 DialPad subscriptions ($21.00/month each)
  • 15 Staff mobile hotspots ($29.40/month each)
  • 40 Student mobile hotspots ($15.00/month each)

 

Total annual subscription is estimated to be $127,539.60.  This total will vary slightly year to year as staff and student needs change.

 

RATIONALE:

School Board Policy DJ requires purchases in excess of $100,000.00 to be approved by the school board.

 

SPECIAL CIRCUMSTANCE(S):

N/A

 

COST BUDGETED:

Total cost for the mobile device services is estimated at $127,539.60 each year.  The requested purchase amount is allocated in the approved budget within the technology cost center.  The purchase will be made using the NASPO MA176 contract with T-Mobile.

 

ALTERNATIVES:

1. Approve the superintendent’s recommendation.

2. Do not approve the superintendent’s recommendation.

BACKGROUND:

The current HVAC system at Matoaka Elementary was installed in 2007 and has reached the end of its useful life. Williamsburg-James City County Public Schools solicited sealed proposals from qualified architecture/engineering firms to produce detailed designs, construction drawings, specifications and contract documents, and to obtain the necessary related regulatory approvals for replacement of the heating ventilating and air conditioning (HVAC) system at Matoaka Elementary School. It is the intent of this proposal to result in the competitive negotiation of a fixed price contract for these services. The successful firm will demonstrate proven management skills and technical competence in the design of HVAC systems of similar design and function. It is anticipated that the required design services will include Architectural and Mechanical, Plumbing, Electrical and Structural Engineering.

 

 The project includes:

  • Replacement of four-pipe induction units, make-up air units, hydronic air handling units, split systems, exhaust fans, air-cooled chillers, boilers, automated HVAC controls, and electrical components associated with the HVAC system.
  • Re-use existing ductwork and piping to the greatest extent possible. Cleaning and sealing of ductwork.

 

An RFP was issued on January 13, 2026, with a due date of March 6, 2026.   Notice of the RFP was posted Euna/OpenBids and placed on the James City County website.

TOPIC:

Discussion of the Fiscal Year 2027 Operating Budget

 

BACKGROUND:

Per the Contract for the Funding of the Joint School System for James City County and Williamsburg City, the School Board agreed that the County's FY 2027 annual contribution to the operational costs of the School System of not less than $104,500,000.00, and the City's FY 2027 annual contribution to the operational costs of the School System of not less than $14,450,000.00. Following receipt of the localities' estimates for their annual contributions to the operational costs of the School System for the following fiscal year, the School Board shall work to develop a needs-based budget within those estimates.

Should the School Board determine that it requires additional funding for the operational costs of the School System in excess of the amounts the localities have provided in their annual operating contribution estimates, they may request an increase in the funding from the County and City in writing specifically (i) asking to receive more than the allocation provides, (ii) describing what the additional funding will be used for, (iii) indicating whether the request is recurring, and (iv) if needed, supporting by resolution, the raising of real property tax or other taxes in the County and/or the City to support their request.

Per school board established budget timeline, the superintendent presented his FY2027 budget to the school board on February 3, 2026.  After the submission of the Superintendent’s Proposed Budget, the school board approved its budget proposal on March 3, 2026; it is then brought before the James City County Board of Supervisors and the Williamsburg City Council for consideration. The localities must approve the appropriation for the school division no later than May 15, 2026 or within 30 days following the completion of the General Assembly session.  Following the approval of the total budget appropriation, the school board makes any necessary adjustments to their budget proposal and adopts a final budget.

 

RATIONALE:

The superintendent presented the recommended budget proposal for the school board’s consideration.   VA §22.1-92, Estimate of moneys needed for public schools; notice of costs to be distributed states, “It shall be the duty of each division superintendent to prepare, with the approval of the school board, and submit to the governing body or bodies appropriating funds for the school division, by the date specified in §15.2-2503, the estimate of the amount of money deemed to be needed during the next fiscal year for the support of the public schools of the school division. The estimate shall set up the amount of money deemed to be needed for each major classification prescribed by the Board of Education and such other headings or items as may be necessary.”

 

The superintendent is charged to prepare the estimate of the amount of funds deemed to be needed during the next fiscal year for the support of the schools in the school division, pursuant to §22.1-92 of the Code of Virginia. Subsequently, the school board is charged with the responsibility to propose, and ultimately adopt, a division budget.
