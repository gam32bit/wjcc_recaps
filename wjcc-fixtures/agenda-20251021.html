Approve Resolution R-21-25, National School Psychology Week

 

BACKGROUND:

The Virginia Department of Education has joined with the Virginia Academy of School Psychologists in celebrating National School Psychology Week, November 3-7, 2025. Schools will highlight the role of school psychology and school-based services in promoting student success.

 

RATIONALE:

School psychologists work with students, general and special educators, parents, administrators, and allied professionals to create safe, healthy, and supportive learning environments focused on collaboration, cooperation, and achievement. School psychologists work to engage students at all levels of learning and development to promote personal achievement, growth, and resilience, as well as a sense of connectedness and wellbeing. The resolution wording is recommended by the National Association for School Psychologists.
