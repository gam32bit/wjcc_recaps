TOPIC:

Review Presentation of the Fiscal Year 27-36 Superintendent’s Proposed Capital Improvement Plan

 

BACKGROUND:

Per school board established budget timeline, the superintendent presents the FY27-36 Capital Improvement Plan (CIP) to the school board.

 

After the submission of the Superintendent’s Proposed CIP, the school board approves the CIP (in December). It is then presented to the James City County Board of Supervisors and Williamsburg City Council for consideration. The localities must approve the CIP and any appropriation for the school division no later than May 15th. Following the approval of the total budget appropriation, the school board makes any necessary adjustments and adopts a final CIP.

 

RATIONALE:

The superintendent is presenting the recommended CIP for the school board’s consideration. VA §22.1-92. Estimate of moneys needed for public schools; notice of costs to be distributed states, “It shall be the duty of each division superintendent to prepare, with the approval of the school board, and submit to the governing body or bodies appropriating funds for the school division, by the date specified in VA §15.2-2503, the estimate of the amount of money deemed to be needed during the next fiscal year for the support of the public schools of the school division. The estimate shall set up the amount of money deemed to be needed for each major classification prescribed by the Board of Education and such other headings or items as may be necessary.”

 

SPECIAL CIRCUMSTANCE:

The documents will be released and available to the public after the board meeting on November 11, 2025.

TOPIC:

Review Award of Contract for Request for Proposal #20250927 Solar Power Purchase Agreement for WJCC Schools to Dominion Energy

 

POLICY ALIGNMENT:

DJ, Purchasing 

DJ-R, Purchasing Regulations

 

BACKGROUND:

In the WJCC Strategic Plan, Elevate '28, Organizational Efficiency & Effectiveness is a priority and guides the work of the Operations Department with respect to optimizing processes.  To support this priority, the Operations team has worked to determine the feasibility of using solar energy for WJCC Schools and facilities. During the analysis, the division determined that partnering with a solar provider would create educational and learning opportunities for students, improve sustainability efforts by reducing energy consumption, and create a cost savings for the school division.

 

WJCC requested proposals from qualified firms, partnerships, corporations, associations or professional organizations to provide turn-key solar Photovoltaics (PV) services through a Power Purchase Agreement (PPA). The division is willing to review other financing and ownership models including a prepaid PPA, a conventional PPA with tax benefits through system donation, a lease, or any other structured proposal. WJCC believes on-site PV power generation will provide a long-term financial benefit by reducing energy costs through reduction of peak demand loads and daily energy consumption at the sites. The program will provide high quality power with minimal environmental impact. The PV System will be a rooftop application on viable WJCC administrative and school buildings.

 

An RFP was issued on March 25, 2025, with a due date of May 6, 2025.  Notice of the RFP was posted on the James City County Bulletin Board, emailed to Contractors, and placed on the James City County website.  

 

RATIONALE:

Seven vendors responded to the RFP.  Of those, Dominion Energy was chosen by the Selection Committee as the respondent that met the needs of WJCC Schools.

 

SPECIAL CIRCUMSTANCE(S):

N/A

 

COST BUDGETED:

There is no cost to WJCCS for installation or maintenance of the solar panels and system. The only cost to WJCC is the cost of purchasing the energy produced by the solar system installed at the respective buildings/facilities.

TOPIC:

School Board Evaluation Tool Discussion

 

POLICY ALIGNMENT: 

AFA, Evaluation of School Board Operational Procedures

 

BACKGROUND:

The school board believes the efficiency of board operations directly affects the efficiency of division operations. Therefore, the board will conduct an annual self-evaluation in December.

 

RATIONALE:

The evaluation will assess the effectiveness of the school board in the discharge of its role, both individually and as a board. The following conditions shall apply to the school board self-evaluation process:

  • School board members are involved in the development of an instrument by which they will evaluate themselves.
  • The school board evaluation will be completed by individual board members confidentially, and submitted to the school board chair or designee, for compilation of individual results.
  • The school board shall meet, with a quorum of members present, to review and discuss the composite opinions and total results.
  • Each conclusion is supported by rationale and objective evidence.

 

SPECIAL CIRCUMSTANCE(S):

Upon completion of the annual process, the school board will consider revisions to the school board self-evaluation process.
