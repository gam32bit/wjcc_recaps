TOPIC:

Approve Resolution R-5-26, National School Social Work Week

 

BACKGROUND:

March is recognized as School Social Work Month and the School Social Work Association of America designates March 1-7, 2026, as National School Social Work Week.  The theme for 2026 is “Unite to Ignite: Celebrating Our Impact.”

 

RATIONALE:

School social workers offer important support services to the division's students and families.  They have expertise in understanding family and community systems and are able to link students with services that are essential for promoting success. School social workers’ training includes preparation in cultural diversity, systems theory, social justice, risk assessment and intervention, consultation, and clinical intervention strategies to address the mental health needs of students.  They work to remove barriers impacting educational performance such as poverty or homelessness and focus on providing supports to vulnerable populations. As such, school social workers deserve to be recognized and appreciated for their work.  Acknowledgement of School Social Work Week will include individual school acknowledgements and recognition by Student Services.

TOPIC:

Discussion of the Fiscal Year 2027 Operating Budget

 

BACKGROUND:

Per the Contract for the Funding of the Joint School System for James City County and Williamsburg City, the School Board agreed that the County's FY 2027 annual contribution to the operational costs of the School System of not less than $104,500,000.00, and the City's FY 2027 annual contribution to the operational costs of the School System of not less than $14,450,000.00. Following receipt of the localities' estimates for their annual contributions to the operational costs of the School System for the following fiscal year, the School Board shall work to develop a needs-based budget within those estimates.

 

Should the School Board determine that it requires additional funding for the operational costs of the School System in excess of the amounts the localities have provided in their annual operating contribution estimates, they may request an increase in the funding from the County and City in writing specifically (i) asking to receive more than the allocation provides, (ii) describing what the additional funding will be used for, (iii) indicating whether the request is recurring, and (iv) if needed, supporting by resolution, the raising of real property tax or other taxes in the County and/or the City to support their request.

 

Per school board established budget timeline, the superintendent presented his FY2027 budget to the school board on February 3, 2026.  After the submission of the Superintendent’s Proposed Budget, the school board approves its budget proposal (in March); it is then brought before the James City County Board of Supervisors and the Williamsburg City Council. The localities must approve the appropriation for the school division no later than May 15, 2026.  Following the approval of the total budget appropriation, the school board makes any necessary adjustments to their budget proposal and adopts a final budget.

 

RATIONALE:

The superintendent has presented the recommended budget proposal for the school board’s consideration.   VA §22.1-92, Estimate of moneys needed for public schools; notice of costs to be distributed states, “It shall be the duty of each division superintendent to prepare, with the approval of the school board, and submit to the governing body or bodies appropriating funds for the school division, by the date specified in §15.2-2503, the estimate of the amount of money deemed to be needed during the next fiscal year for the support of the public schools of the school division. The estimate shall set up the amount of money deemed to be needed for each major classification prescribed by the Board of Education and such other headings or items as may be necessary.”
