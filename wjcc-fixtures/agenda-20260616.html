TOPIC:

Approve Authorization for VHSL Memberships for Jamestown, Lafayette and Warhill High Schools for the 2026-2027 School Year

 

POLICY ALIGNMENT:

IGD, Extracurricular Activities

IGDJ, Interscholastic Athletics

 

BACKGROUND:

In 1984, the Virginia High School League (VHSL) voted to require member schools to obtain catastrophic insurance through the League. The Executive Committee voted at that time to require the division superintendent’s signature on the membership form, and later unanimously voted to request that the school board chair also sign the membership form. The purpose of the signature is to indicate that each board approves its schools’ VHSL membership, reinforcing the position that schools join with the expressed written consent of their school board.

 

The League has accepted a proposal for student accident and liability insurance coverage with VACORP. The insurance assessment to members for the 2026–2027 school year is $5.50 per student. These proceeds are used to pay premiums on the insurance policies that the VHSL must maintain in order to conduct its programs and activities. The policy has a $25,000 deductible with a $3 million lifetime excess medical expense benefit limit.

 

The 2026–2027 membership fee is $800 plus $35 for each varsity activity.

 

RATIONALE:

High school activities, affiliated with the VHSL, provide numerous benefits for students, schools, and the community-at-large in Williamsburg and James City County. Studies have shown that student athletes perform better academically than their peers, are less likely to participate in unhealthy or risky behaviors, and manage time more efficiently. Participation leads to leadership skills, positive social connections, and goal setting. VHSL athletics and activities not only foster a strong sense of school spirit, but often create a strong sense of school community pride as well.

 

SPECIAL CIRCUMSTANCE(S):

Membership application fees are due in the VHSL office prior to August 1, 2026. After August 1, schools are charged a 10 percent late payment penalty.

 

COST BUDGETED:

The VHSL membership fee for each school is paid from the operating budget of the individual school:

Jamestown High School    $4,420

Lafayette High School       $4,440

Warhill High School           $4,640

Goals
Supporting Priority: Safety & Security - Ensure a fully integrated approach to safety and security that encompasses both the physical environment and the social/emotional needs of students.

TOPIC:

Approve Purchase Request for Middle School Athletic Training Services from Sentara for an Annual Cost of $116,000

 

BACKGROUND:

In the adopted FY27 budget, funding was included to expand athletic trainer coverage to all four middle schools.  This investment enhances the health and safety of student athletes by ensuring timely injury prevention, evaluation and response during practices and competitions.

RATIONALE: 
Athletic trainers play an essential role in protecting student athletes and supporting the daily operations of WJCC’s athletic programs.     Goals
    Supporting Priority: Communications & Engagement - Cultivate a culture of open and effective communication to inform and engage all stakeholders.

    TOPIC:

    Redistricting Process Update

     

    BACKGROUND:

    Many school buildings in WJCC Schools, specifically at the elementary level, are at capacity. To accommodate student population, eight of nine WJCC elementary schools have portable classrooms (trailers) on site. With the anticipated August 2027 occupancy of two new pre-k centers, the school division must also consider the best and most efficient way to utilize elementary space that will be available when pre-k students are located in the new pre-k spaces as the current pre-k program is housed in five elementary schools. The shift will leave some elementary spaces unoccupied while other spaces remain at or over capacity. Engaging in conversation on school zones also allows the school division to consider boundaries, feeder pattern alignment, efficiencies and effective delivery at the secondary level. 

     

    RATIONALE:

    The Redistricting Steering Committee released draft attendance zone maps for each level on May 12, 2026. Community members have engaged in a feedback process. Administration will present an update on the redistricting process and recommendations for the Redistricting Steering Committee to consider in its next map iteration. A timeline of next steps will be shared.
