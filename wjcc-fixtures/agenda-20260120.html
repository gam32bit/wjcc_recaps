Approve the Financial Report and Monthly Bills & Payroll for the Month Ended November 30, 2025

 

BACKGROUND:

The Finance Department prepares financial reports and a monthly bills & payroll report at the end of each month. These reports summarize the financial activities of the school division. The reports prepared include:

1.    Financial Highlights

2.    Budget Status Report – All Funds

3.    Expenditures by Cost Center – Operating Fund

4.    Monthly Bills & Payroll – All Funds

5.    Purchase Orders issued over $25,000

6.    Contracts Signed between $25,000 - $100,000

7.    Transfers Between Categories up to $25,000

 

RATIONALE:

The presentation of the monthly financial reports and monthly bills & payroll report ensures accountability to the board and the citizens of James City County and the City of Williamsburg of the financial resources entrusted to the school division.

None
