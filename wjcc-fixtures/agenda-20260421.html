TOPIC:

Resolution R-14-26, National School Nurse Day

 

BACKGROUND:

National School Nurse Day was established in 1977 to foster a better understanding of the role of school nurses in the educational setting. Williamsburg-James City County Public School Division recognizes our school nurses for the delivery of professional school health services to WJCC students each day. National School Nurse Day is celebrated on May 6, 2026, as a part of National Nurse Week.

 

RATIONALE:

Public Schools employ 18 school nurses, all of which are registered nurses, that have the expertise and training to assess and assist the school child, the child’s family, and the educational team in providing appropriate school health services that can promote educational success. Our registered nurses’ primary concern is for the health and safety of our students. They are responsible for nursing assessment, problem identification, prevention, intervention, planning and outcome evaluation which requires independent nursing, WJCC judgment, as well as emergency care and medication administration. They are responsible for functioning according to Virginia’s nursing practice act and rulings of the board of nursing, which stipulate scope of practice.
