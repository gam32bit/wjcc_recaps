_**RATIONALE:**_

Childhood bullying is a significant problem nationwide. It can cause school absenteeism, mental and physical stress, poor school performance, and poor self-esteem. School board members, superintendents, teachers, and parents can play a critical role in creating a climate where bullying is not tolerated. Each October, WJCC Public Schools supports Bullying Prevention Month through a variety of activities. The division website is utilized to provide information on bullying prevention and the October observance. Each school plans specific activities to acknowledge Bullying Prevention Month.

**_TOPIC:_**

Approve Resolution R-19-25, National School Lunch Week

 

**_BACKGROUND:_**

First declared in 1962, the annual observance of National School Lunch Week promotes the importance of a healthy school lunch in a child's life and the impact it has inside and outside of the classroom.  

 

The National School Lunch Program (NSLP) serves nearly 30 million children every school day to ensure the health and well-being of all public school students, boost academic performance by giving students the fuel they need to succeed, and closed the opportunity gap for children across the country.  The program reduces childhood hunger and also supports American agriculture.

 

**_RATIONALE:_**

The WJCC Child Nutrition Services Program provides lunch and breakfast to students daily, while adhering to strict nutrition standards and navigating student food allergies. Research demonstrates a strong relationship between nutrition and readiness to learn. The meals served to students provide the energy and nutrients for optimal cognitive development and performance. Williamsburg-James City County has exceptional Child Nutrition Services staff members in each of the sixteen schools, and the division is proud to recognize the difference school nutrition professionals make for every child who comes through the cafeteria. 

 

The week of October 13-17, 2025, is recognized as National School Lunch Week.

The program will provide educators with a tool that searches the Internet, as well as databases of papers and digital texts for sources of potential plagiarism. The tool would then generate a custom report that highlights and provides links to any textual matches found on the Internet, in databases of previously submitted papers, and/or in proprietary databases of subscription-based publication material. The desire of this program is to support the schools’ efforts for identifying academic integrity and to provide teachers with the resources to have conversations about academic writing.

  1. Online Originality Detective Service
  2. Similarity Search Database

  4. Formative Writing Tool
  5. System Reporting
  6. Certain Technical Requirements
  7. Data Privacy and Security
  8. Product Training and Support
  9. AI Detection Requirements



 

The proposals were reviewed based on the following evaluation criteria:

 

  * Vendor Qualifications, Experience / References
  * Demonstrated understanding of the Statement of Needs and ability to meet RFP requirements
  * Functionality of the Software
  * Proposed Work Plan that demonstrates the firm’s ability to provide services
  * Cost



The contract will be for an initial two (2) year period and eight (8) optional one-year renewals.  

A request for proposal was issued on May 15, 2025, with a due date of June 12, 2025.  Notice of the RFP was posted on the James City County website, posted on the Commonwealth of Virginia eProcurement Portal website, and posted on Demandstar Procurement Portal website. _**RATIONALE:  **_

The school board may make additional appointments to an advisory committee during the school year.  After committees are formed, any additional applications received will be saved for review and consideration either in the event of mid-year vacancies, or for possible appointment the following school year.
