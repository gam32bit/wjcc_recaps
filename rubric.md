# Item Importance Rubric

Score each agenda item 0–5 based on how much it matters to WJCC families,
students, staff, and taxpayers. Apply the criteria below literally and
consistently. The score supplements measurable signals (discussion time,
dollar amounts) — it is not the primary ranking.

---

## Scoring criteria

**5 — High impact, affects many people, hard to reverse**
- Adopts or substantially changes the annual operating/capital budget
- Changes what most students learn or how they are taught (curriculum adoption
  for a core subject, graduation requirements, major program creation/elimination)
- Changes pay, benefits, or employment terms for a significant portion of staff
- Commits large public money over multiple years (multi-year contracts, leases,
  major capital purchases)
- Approves or revokes a policy that directly affects student safety or welfare

**4 — Meaningful impact but more limited in scope**
- Approves a single-year purchase that is large relative to typical budget items
  (e.g., division-wide technology refresh, major facilities project)
- Changes a policy that affects how families interact with schools (discipline,
  enrollment, transportation, fees)
- Creates a new program or significantly restructures an existing one
- Affects a specific school's staffing model or schedule in a material way

**3 — Moderate, specific impact**
- Adopts curriculum or instructional materials for a specific course or grade
  level
- Approves a contract or purchase that is notable but not large in system-wide
  terms
- Changes a policy with practical but limited day-to-day effect on most families
- Grant funding or budget amendment that creates or expands a specific program

**2 — Low direct impact on most residents**
- Routine policy housekeeping (renaming, cross-referencing, or aligning to state
  code with no substantive change)
- Small purchase or contract renewal with no change in scope
- Personnel actions that are administrative in nature (routine appointments,
  routine retirements)

**1 — Procedural or informational with no decision**
- Information/discussion item with no vote
- Approval of a report or plan that contains no funding or policy change
- Recognitions, spotlights, or announcements carried over from boilerplate

**0 — Should have been dropped as boilerplate but wasn't**
- Truly procedural with no substance whatsoever
- Item is a duplicate or error

---

## Notes for the scorer

- When in doubt between two scores, prefer the lower one.
- "Annual" or "routine" items can still score 3–5 if the dollar amount or
  scope is large (e.g., the annual budget adoption scores 5 even though it
  happens every year).
- A consent agenda item can score 3–5 if its substance is significant — consent
  placement reflects procedural efficiency, not importance.
- Ignore the board's rhetoric about an item; score the substance.
